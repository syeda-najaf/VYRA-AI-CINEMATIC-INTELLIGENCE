Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026. Starting template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed for GDPR / DPDP / CCPA as relevant to your users.

What we collect

Your prompts, any photo you choose to animate, the videos generated, a random device ID used for daily limits and ownership of your videos, and your IP address for abuse prevention and rate limiting. We do not require an account.

How we use it

To generate your video, enforce fair-use limits, keep the service safe and fix problems. We do not sell your data.

Who processes it

Prompts and photos are sent to AI providers (fal.ai and, for voices, ElevenLabs; optionally Hugging Face) to create your video. Those providers have their own privacy policies.

Retention

Uploaded photos are not stored by VYRA after the video is made. Generated videos and thumbnails are deleted after 7 days or when you delete them. Your library list is stored on your own device.

Your choices

Delete any video from your Library at any time. To ask about your data, email support@vyra.ai.

Children

VYRA is not directed to children under 13.
